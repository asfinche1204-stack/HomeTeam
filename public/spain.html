Good to know

  • Spain eats late. Lunch runs about 1:30–3:30, dinner from 8:30. Kitchens often close in between, so plan tapas bars or markets for the gap.
  • Menú del día at lunch (two courses, bread, drink) is the best value meal of the day.
  • 3–4 hours of walking on tour days, much of it cobblestone. Broken-in shoes matter more than anything else you pack.
  • Two long coach days (to Valencia and the ride down to Andalusia). Keep snacks, a charger and a layer in the day bag, not the suitcase.
  • Pickpockets work the Madrid metro, Las Ramblas and around Sagrada Família. Zipped crossbody in front, phone off the table.
  • Tipping is light: round up or leave 5–10% for a sit-down meal you loved.
  • Plugs are Type C/F at 230V. Phone and laptop chargers are fine with a plug adapter; check hair tools for dual voltage.
